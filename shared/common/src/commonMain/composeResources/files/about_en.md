## Virtual Currency and No Cash Value

All prices and winnings within this application are purely fictional and for entertainment purposes only. The currency used in our games is **"Liras,"** which is a virtual currency and not real money.

You acknowledge and agree that **"Liras"**:
* Have no monetary or "real world" value.
* Cannot be redeemed or exchanged for real money, cash prizes, or any other items of monetary value from us or any other party.
* Cannot be sold, traded, or transferred to other users.

Any language used in the app, such as *"winnings,"* *"prizes,"* or *"cash,"* refers strictly to virtual **"Liras"** and does not imply any real-world payout or value. Your virtual "Liras" balance does not constitute a real-world balance or reflect any stored value.
