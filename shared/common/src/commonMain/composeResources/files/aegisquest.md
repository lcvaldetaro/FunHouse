# Space StationAegis

            

### Version 1.0 - Complete the Mission!

        
        
        

---

        

Welcome Cadet to the Aegis Prime Space Station. The station is under siege by three opposing factions: Solar Vanguard, Xenophage Swarm, and Mecha-Legion. Your goal is to complete the objective assigned to you at the start of the mission.

        

**💡 Survival Tips:**

        * Several rooms on the station are pitch black. You must pick up a **flashlight** and use the **TURN FLASHLIGHT ON** command to explore and interact in these areas.

        * Blast doors are locked. Use the **OPEN** or **UNLOCK** command while carrying the appropriate keycard to pass through.

## Game Commands

Interact with the world using simple 1 or 2 word commands:

### Movement
Move between locations using cardinal directions:
* **north** (**n**), **south** (**s**), **east** (**e**), **west** (**w**)
* **northeast** (**ne**), **northwest** (**nw**), **southeast** (**se**), **southwest** (**sw**)
* **up** (to ascend decks), **down** (to descend decks), **in**, **out**, **inside**, **outside**, **enter**, **move**, **go**

### Observation
* **look** (or **?**) - Re-describe the current room, showing exits and visible items.
* **where** - Find out where other players are.

### Item Management
* **get** / **take** / **grab** *<item>* - Pick up an item.
* **drop** / **leave** *<item>* - Drop an item in the room.

### Interactions
* **open** / **unlock** *<door>* - Open a closed blast door or chest.
* **close** *<door>* - Close a container.
* **start** *<console>* - Activate reactor consoles, escape pod launches, or computer cores.
* **turn** *<flashlight> on/off* - Toggle devices like flashlights on or off.
* **repair** / **fix** *<module>* - Fix station subsystems.
* **fight** / **kill** / **battle** *<target>* - Fight hostile droids or organisms.
* **read** *<object>* - Read datapads, notes, or launch codes.
* **ask** *<character>* - Talk to station personnel or droids.

### System
* **inventory** (or **i**) - View the list of items in your carrying slots.
* **score** - Show your current achievements and points.
* **goal** (or **goals**) - Display your active mission goal.
* **players** - List all the current players in the game.
* **save** - Save your progress.
* **restore** / **load** - Reload your last saved game state.
* **newhandle** *<nickname>* - Change player name and randomize character roles.
* **help** - Display this command helper window.
* **quit** - Exit the game.
