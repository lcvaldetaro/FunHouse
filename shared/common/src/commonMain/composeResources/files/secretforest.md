Secret Of The Forest

```
    This is a text adventure game engine, and comes with a finished game that is
    immediately playable. Features in the game (all viewable on the help screen)
    include:
```

    walk        navigate around the world

    look        examine surroundings or item

    search      find hidden items

    take        pick up item

    drop        drop item

    use     use item or items

In the game are 33 rooms, 22 items, and 45 interactions.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

HOW TO PLAY

Explore the world and solve the mystery of the forest!

To perform an action, use the following commands:

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

NAVIGATING THE WORLD

walk direction (or just direction) - move in the direction specified

look - examine your surroundings

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

MANAGING ITEMS

inventory - list your belongings

take - pick up an item

drop - drop an item

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

INTERACTING WITH THE WORLD

search - look for hidden objects

use - use an item or items

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

TO QUIT

quit - give up in your search and go home

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
