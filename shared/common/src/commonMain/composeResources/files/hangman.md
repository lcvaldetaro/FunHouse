HANGMAN

The game itself is pretty well known. This version, however, gives points in the form
of "Liras"; they are earned this way: When you play a word, you get 100 Liras;
you get 12 possibilities of errors for each word, every time you make an error, 10 Liras
are deducted from your account. If you lose (get "hanged"), you lose 100 Liras. If you
win, you get 25 Liras. If you leave the game before guessing the word, you lose 100
Liras.

If you need a clue about the word, type the command "meaning"; It will give you the dictionary
definition for the word, and will cost you 25 Liras

if you want to decide which word to guess (for a friend to guess), you can type
the command "word xxxxxx", where "xxxxxx" is the word to be guessed. You then give your phone
for your friend to try.
